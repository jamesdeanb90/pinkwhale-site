01 Our Story

No pitch deck. Just what we've built, one step at a time.

  1. 4,000

    It started on TikTok.

    Plain-English crypto videos found 4,000 followers who wanted the no-jargon version.

  2. LIVE

    Then came live stream nights.

    We hang out, read charts, ask the "dumb" questions and learn out loud.

  3. ~200

    The Telegram crew showed up.

    The Pink Whale Community grew to a couple hundred people sharing news, memes and help.

  4. ~50

    The Discord became a classroom.

    About 50 of us work through crypto education PDFs, step by step. Educational, not financial advice.

  5. 15

    Then we built our own app.

    Pink Pod is our home base, and it already has 15 members.

Launched on Pump.funFilled the bonding curveMoved to PumpSwapLiquidity locked forever

That's the moment Chill Whale Token stopped being just a meme and became infrastructure.

The bonding curve is Pump.fun's starter price ramp. When it fills, the coin graduates to a real trading pool, and the pool's LP tokens (the keys to its liquidity) are burned, so nobody can pull that liquidity out.

  4. Open the wallet section and switch to Solana.
